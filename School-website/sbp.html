About Our School

Knowledge is Power, Education is Life

Introduction

Shree Byraweshwara Educational and Development Foundation is a well-established educational institution dedicated to providing quality education rooted in strong values, discipline, and holistic development. Guided by the timeless belief that “Knowledge is Power”, the school strives to shape young minds into confident, responsible, and capable individuals.

Located in Belagola, Mandya, the school was founded with a special focus on educating students from rural areas. Established under the trusted guidance of the Hombaiyya Trust, Indra Convent began its journey in 2004, followed by the establishment of Shree Byraweshware Public School in 2011, with the vision of making quality education accessible and affordable for all.

Our institution believes that every child possesses unique talents and abilities. Through a nurturing and disciplined environment, we focus on academic excellence, moral values, life skills, and character building. By combining traditional values with modern teaching practices, we prepare students to meet the challenges of the future with confidence and integrity.

Equal emphasis is given to co-curricular and extracurricular activities to ensure the all-round development of students. Sports, cultural programs, yoga, environmental awareness activities, and leadership opportunities are an integral part of the learning experience, helping students develop confidence, teamwork, and creativity.

Our team of dedicated and experienced educators is committed to nurturing young minds through innovative teaching methods.

Mission & Vision

Mission:
The mission of Shree Byraweshwara Educational and Development Foundation is to provide high-quality English-medium education with a strong value-based foundation, especially for students from rural and semi-rural backgrounds. We are committed to nurturing academic excellence, discipline, ethical values, and social responsibility in a safe and supportive learning environment.

We aim to empower students with critical thinking abilities, effective communication skills, creativity, and self-confidence. By fostering curiosity, respect, and lifelong learning, we prepare our students to succeed academically and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our mission also includes promoting inclusive education, ensuring equal learning opportunities for every child, and encouraging innovation in teaching practices. We strive to create a learning culture that motivates students to explore their talents, develop leadership qualities, and embrace lifelong personal growth.

Vision:
Our vision is to develop self-reliant, confident, and morally strong individuals who are capable of making wise decisions and leading with integrity. We aspire to be a center of excellence in rural education, inspiring students to realize their full potential and become responsible citizens who positively impact the nation and the world.

We envision an educational environment where innovation meets tradition, technology enhances learning, and values guide every action. By continuously improving academic standards and infrastructure, we aim to shape future-ready students who are adaptable, compassionate, and globally aware.
