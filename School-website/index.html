About Our School

Better Alternative for Better Career

Year of Establishment: 2016 – 2017

Introduction

Pluspoint Aksharadhama School is a progressive educational institution committed to academic excellence, personal growth, and character development. Established with the vision of delivering quality education, the school focuses on nurturing knowledge, discipline, and confidence in every student.

We believe education is a lifelong journey that goes beyond textbooks and examinations. Our approach encourages students to think critically, ask meaningful questions, and develop strong problem-solving abilities while upholding ethical values and respect for others.

In a supportive and inclusive learning environment, every child is guided to discover their unique strengths and talents. Our dedicated educators play a vital role in shaping confident, curious, and socially responsible learners prepared for future challenges.

Alongside academics, we emphasize discipline, communication skills, leadership, and personal responsibility—ensuring students are well-prepared for higher education and real-world success.

With a balanced blend of traditional values and modern teaching methodologies, our school fosters creativity, innovation, and a love for learning. We aim to instill lifelong learning habits that help students adapt and excel in a rapidly evolving world.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission:
Our mission is to provide high-quality education that promotes academic excellence, character building, and holistic development. We strive to create a disciplined, supportive, and engaging learning environment where every student receives equal opportunities to grow intellectually, emotionally, and socially.

Through dedicated teaching, modern learning practices, and value-based education, we aim to empower students with critical thinking skills, creativity, effective communication, and a strong sense of responsibility toward society.

We are committed to nurturing ethical values, mutual respect, and cultural awareness, ensuring students develop integrity and compassion alongside academic achievement.

Vision:
Our vision is to develop confident, self-motivated, and independent learners who possess leadership qualities and strong decision-making abilities. We aspire to nurture future-ready individuals who contribute positively to their communities and succeed in an ever-changing global environment.

We envision a school community where innovation, inclusivity, and excellence inspire students to reach their full potential and become responsible global citizens.

School News & Achievements

Celebrating the remarkable accomplishments of our students and faculty

Experiential Learning
Pluspoint Market – Learning Buyers & Sellers

Through the Pluspoint Market activity, students learn about real-world buying and selling by observing and interacting with vegetable shop owners and street vendors. This experience helps them understand the roles of buyers and sellers in a local market environment.

Cultural Celebration
Kannada Rajyotsava Celebration

Our school celebrated Kannada Rajyotsava with great enthusiasm, honoring the rich heritage, language, and culture of Karnataka. Students participated in cultural programs, speeches, and activities that fostered pride, unity, and respect for regional traditions.

Martial Arts
Karate Belt Promotion – White to Yellow Belt

Our students successfully progressed from White Belt to Yellow Belt in karate, demonstrating improved discipline, focus, and basic self-defense techniques. This achievement reflects their consistent practice, confidence, and commitment to physical fitness and personal development.
