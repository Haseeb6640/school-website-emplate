About Our School

Knowledge is Power, Education is Life

Introduction

Indra Convent is a well-established educational institution dedicated to providing quality education rooted in strong values, discipline, and holistic development. Guided by the timeless belief that “Knowledge is Power”, the school strives to shape young minds into confident, responsible, and capable individuals.

Located in Belagola, Mandya, the school was founded with a special focus on educating students from rural areas. Established under the trusted guidance of the Hombaiyya Trust, Indra Convent began its journey in 2004, followed by the establishment of Shree Byraweshware Public School in 2011, with the vision of making quality education accessible and affordable for all.

Our institution believes that every child possesses unique talents and abilities. Through a nurturing and disciplined environment, we focus on academic excellence, moral values, life skills, and character building. By combining traditional values with modern teaching practices, we prepare students to meet the challenges of the future with confidence and integrity.

Indra Convent emphasizes the importance of a balanced education that integrates academics with co-curricular and extracurricular activities. Sports, cultural programs, yoga, and creative arts are encouraged to help students develop confidence, teamwork, leadership skills, and emotional well-being.

Our experienced and dedicated faculty members are committed to guiding students with care, patience, and professionalism. By adopting innovative teaching methodologies, continuous assessment, and individual attention, we ensure that every child receives the support needed to achieve academic success.

Mission & Vision

Mission:
The mission of Indra Convent is to provide high-quality English-medium education with a strong value-based foundation, especially for students from rural and semi-rural backgrounds. We are committed to nurturing academic excellence, discipline, ethical values, and social responsibility in a safe and supportive learning environment.

We aim to empower students with critical thinking abilities, effective communication skills, creativity, and self-confidence. By fostering curiosity, respect, and lifelong learning, we prepare our students to succeed academically and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our mission also focuses on creating an inclusive and student-centered learning environment that encourages innovation, collaboration, and personal growth. We strive to identify and nurture individual talents while promoting leadership qualities and a sense of social responsibility among students.

Vision:
Our vision is to develop self-reliant, confident, and morally strong individuals who are capable of making wise decisions and leading with integrity. We aspire to be a center of excellence in rural education, inspiring students to realize their full potential and become responsible citizens who positively impact the nation and the world.

We envision an institution where academic excellence is balanced with character building, cultural awareness, and technological advancement.
